## Key Facts for Content Generation

### Company snapshot

Smartivate — Karlsruhe, Germany. Industry: IoT, Software & Energy Efficiency. Backed by EXIST-Gründerstipendium, Axel Accelerator, SAP Up2B, Burda Bootcamp.

### Problem

* 70+ manufacturers and 600+ smart home products in Germany alone — finding compatible ones is "a needle in a stack"
* 6+ communication standards (Zigbee, Z-Wave, WiFi, Bluetooth, LoRaWAN, NFC) causing incompatibility and customer frustration
* Finding products that fit home attributes, budget & preferences can take days or weeks
* Customers unknowingly buy incompatible products and end up juggling a dozen different apps

### Solution

Configurator as a Service for online shops, affiliate platforms and the housing industry. Combines a Smart Products Database with a Product Compatibility Mapping System (PCMS) algorithm to deliver tailor-made, cross-brand smart home packages in under 20 minutes.

### Process flow (5 steps) — useful for the Customer Support/User Guide

1. Choose your Central Hub based on existing infrastructure
2. Select between Efficiency, Security and Comfort
3. Hand-pick product categories based on preferences and home attributes
4. Decide on products from tailor-made packages (best rating / best price / recommended / custom)
5. Add products to the basket, shop and enjoy

### Benefits — B2B (for sales content)

* Increase in basket size
* Upselling and cross-selling potential
* Increase in conversion rates
* Fewer returns due to incompatibilities between devices
* Faster ROI and maximal benefit

### Benefits — End user (for marketing content)

* Product variety across 70+ brands
* Tailor-made solutions based on preferences and home attributes
* Cross-branded product bundling
* Profile-based Applet suggestions
* Guided selling

### Competitive differentiation

Unlike Busch-Jaeger, devolo, Somfy or Conrad Connect, Smartivate is the only one combining product variety, tailor-made solutions, cross-branding, profile-based suggestions and guided selling in a single configurator.

### Market & traction (credibility proof points)

* German smart home market potential: €22.2bn (Splendid Research, Smart Home Monitor 2017)
* Adoption grew from 12.4% (2012/13) to 36.1% (2018/19) of households
* Winner, SmartHome Deutschland Award 2019
* Winner, EEHack 2019; Top 10, A1 Telekom IoT Challenge; Winner, Hubitation Accelerator
* Pilot projects with Nassauische Heimstätte/Wohnstadt (real estate) and Home&Smart (affiliate platform)

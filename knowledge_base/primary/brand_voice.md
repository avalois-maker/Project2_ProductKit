## Key Facts for Content Generation

### Descriptor

"The Configurator for Plug & Play Smart Home Devices"

### Positioning

Germany's first cross-brand Smart Home Configurator — compatible, tailor-made solutions based on budget, preferences and home attributes.

### Promise

"Making your home smart has never been easier!"

### Tone principles

* **Reassuring, not technical** — lead with the outcome, not the protocol
* **Warm and personal** — use "your home", "your preferences"; sign-off is "Made with love for great people"
* **Concrete** — name the time saved, the products, the steps
* **Bilingual** — EN and DE must read as native, not translated

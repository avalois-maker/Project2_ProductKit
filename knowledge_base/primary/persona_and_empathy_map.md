# Persona & Empathy Map

*Source: Persona & Empathy Map.pdf (Design Thinking, 12.11.2015)*

## Persona: Fred Müller

- **Name & Age:** Fred Müller, 36 years old
- **Family & Social Status:** In a relationship, middle class — living with partner
- **City:** Karlsruhe, Germany
- **Job & Income:** Tax Consultant, avg. €2,500/month
- **Role & Position in Stakeholder Network:** Full-time professional. Lives independently and manages his expenses comfortably.
- **Hobbies & Identifiers:** Plays guitar and listens to music, likes spending time with family at home, likes inviting friends over.

### Pain Points & Needs / Attitude Towards SmartHome Products
He is a tenant in a shared apartment. Has little or no information on SmartHome products. He feels insecure due to the fear of hacking. He thinks he needs assistance with installation.

### Goals & Dreams
Quite interested in attaining energy efficiency, especially resulting in cost savings. Looks forward to living in a very secure and comfortable house.

## Empathy Map

**SAY**
- Not aware / not well-informed of SmartHomes (SHs)
- They already have taken efficient measures in lighting
- Prefer to buy in a familiar online platform or a regular store

**THINK**
- They may be interested in making their homes smart
- There is a lot of technology and expertise needed
- Their data will no more be safe

**DO**
- Switching to LEDs
- Don't use a lot of heating
- Rely on friends' and relatives' opinions & experiences

**FEEL**
- It is necessary to feel safe and comfortable
- It is better to buy as a combo package of products
- It is luxury & is expensive
